# Code of conduct

AERONEX is built for students, hobbyists and engineers learning to build and fly drones safely.
Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to drones or to open source.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and explain the reason behind it.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Put flight safety first: point out a risk plainly, and never encourage unsafe or illegal flying.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Help with using drones to harm, spy on or endanger people.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and anywhere someone represents
the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer, Dwij Vyas, at [dwijgvyas@gmail.com](mailto:dwijgvyas@gmail.com).
Reports are read promptly and kept confidential.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block anyone who breaks this code,
for as long as is needed to keep the project a good place to work. Where it helps, the person is
told why first.
